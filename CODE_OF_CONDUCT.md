# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Tanzania Missing Persons Registry a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community — and to remember that behind every record in this registry is a missing person and a family searching for answers. We treat them, and everyone who works on this project, with dignity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors and toward the families affected by the cases documented here
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the accuracy and safety of the record, not just for ourselves as individual contributors

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information — including the identities of anonymous report submitters or at-risk family members — without explicit permission
- Political grandstanding, hate speech, or defamatory statements in case records, issues, or discussions
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, records, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — including the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
